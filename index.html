    Compliance

    Compliance is more than filing on time.

    Filing is the visible part. Good compliance is the system underneath: the one that holds up when a notice arrives or an auditor asks for three years of records.

    How a filing holds up
    1. Correct inputs and wage computation
    2. Filed within the statutory window
    3. Challan and acknowledgement archived
    4. Registers and returns kept current
    5. Reviewed and owned, every cycle
