Statutory Compliance Management

Establishment registration and employee onboarding with EPFO & ESIC portals
Monthly PF ECR filing, ESIC contribution returns, and branch-wise compliance

Payroll Management

Multi-branch consolidated payroll with branch-wise reporting
Strict data confidentiality protocols across all client engagements
