The problem

HR shouldn't feel like a maze.

Most growing businesses carry HR, payroll and compliance across spreadsheets, portals, consultants and memory. The work recurs, the deadlines don't move, and the rules keep changing.

  • Deadlines on every calendar

    PF, ESI, PT and LWF each run on their own schedule, in every state you operate.

  • Payroll that must reconcile

    Salary structures, LOP, arrears and deductions have to tie out, every month.

  • Rules that keep shifting

    Circulars, rate revisions and the Labour Codes land with little warning.

  • Paperwork that goes stale

    Offer letters, records and registrations pile up and fall out of date.

  • Contract labour duties

    Contractors bring their own registers, returns and principal-employer obligations.

  • Notices out of nowhere

    One missed filing can turn into inspections, queries and penalties.

  • Manual re-keying

    Copying data between systems is slow and quietly error-prone.

  • No single picture

    The full view lives in too many places to see at once.

HR Terrain maps the whole landscape: operations, payroll and compliance under one framework.

Compliance

Compliance is more than filing on time.

Filing is the visible part. Good compliance is the system underneath: the one that holds up when a notice arrives or an auditor asks for three years of records.

How a filing holds up
  1. Correct inputs and wage computation
  2. Filed within the statutory window
  3. Challan and acknowledgement archived
  4. Registers and returns kept current
  5. Reviewed and owned, every cycle
